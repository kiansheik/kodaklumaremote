LUMA Remote 0.4.12

Scrubbing is rebuilt. Touch the bar and a large preview appears at once; fast drags move 1:1, and holding slowly for a moment switches to finer control (1/3, then 1/10). Thumbnails are generated in the background from the moment you cast, middle first and then ever finer down to one every 3 seconds, kept for 48 hours and shared with other phones through the projector. The projector shows the same preview from its own copy. Fixes the crash when holding the scrub bar.

Undo and Redo arrows beside the bar jump between the places you scrubbed from, per episode, and survive restarting the app. The refresh button is gone; playback stays current on its own.

Saved positions now resume correctly on LUMA Receiver.

LUMA Receiver can continue a series on its own: Next episode now works even after an episode has finished or when the phone reconnects later, and an optional Auto-play next episode switch plays the following episode in the subtitle language you last turned on (trying another file in that language if one fails). After three episodes without anyone touching a remote it stops, and the app shows which episode you last actually interacted with.

The Remote tab has a cleaner layout: what is playing, the seek bar and play controls sit together in one card, then volume and navigation, with Subtitles, Auto-play and More along the bottom. It is ready sooner after opening the app, and shows the connect screen in its place until a projector is connected and paired.

Resume and replays start faster: a title played in the last few hours skips the catalog lookup when its stream still answers.
