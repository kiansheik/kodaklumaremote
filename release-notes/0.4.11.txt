LUMA Remote 0.4.11

Casting no longer waits for the projector's native navigation service during startup. The phone connects directly to LUMA Receiver as soon as its boot-time service and saved private address are available, so a video can start while the rest of the projector is still coming online.

Projector navigation, apps, volume and power reconnect separately when the native protocol becomes available. If that connection drops later, receiver playback, casting and media controls remain available while hardware-only controls are hidden.
