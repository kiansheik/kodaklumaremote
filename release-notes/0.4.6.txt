LUMA Remote 0.4.6

Adds a direct phone-hosted Philips TV receiver with stream playback, Portuguese-first subtitle selection, playback controls, and a movie/series/episode browser.

The new Library keeps watched movie/series metadata and per-episode progress privately on your phone, available offline without saving media files. Choose Resume, Start over or a known next episode on Philips or the LUMA/Kodi projector. Sources are resolved again when selected; media links are not stored in viewing history.

Adds receiver shortcuts, a separate TV power-on action, remote page reload, and optional developer diagnostics. The TV page supports directional keys and pointer-style remote input with a hidden page cursor.

On the tested Philips 43PFG6910/78, HLS picture, sound and Portuguese captions are user-confirmed. Catalog browsing, cross-device resume and the latest physical-remote navigation changes pass local checks but still need end-to-end testing on that TV. Last-source startup is configured; standby wake and automatic browser restoration are not yet verified. The phone must keep hosting the receiver.
