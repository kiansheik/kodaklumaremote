LUMA Remote 0.4.24

Stream local MP4, MKV and WebM files from the phone to LUMA Receiver, with external SRT, WebVTT, ASS, SSA and TTML subtitles. A laptop can also stream original or high-quality compatibility-converted media from a localhost browser remote. Other paired phones can control playback, scrub and reuse the shared thumbnail previews.

Anime playback now retains verified series, season, episode and Sub/Dub identity through Library, Resume, Next and auto-play. Library items can be deleted with synchronized tombstones, and movie sources that positively identify a different title are rejected in favor of another server.

Remote can open the current title's exact watch page without interrupting projector playback, making it easier to choose another stream before casting it.

LUMA Receiver 0.3.7 supports opaque local-media sessions, shared scrub thumbnails and faster, time-prioritized buffering for high-bitrate media. It can be selected as the projector Home app and reports startup/Home-input diagnostics for setup.
