Adds Philips TV remote pairing with a QR scanner or manual Matter setup code. This phone's pairing is saved with Android Keystore encryption and reconnects when the app opens.

Adds supported TV navigation, speaker volume, input selection and confirmed power-off controls. “Add another phone” creates a temporary QR/setup code without removing existing phones.

LUMA/Kodi and the Philips remote keep separate connections. Video and external subtitles still require a compatible playback app; Matter remote control does not add video-URL playback to this TV.

Requires 64-bit ARM Android 7 or newer.
