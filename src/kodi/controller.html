Kodi receiver test

Send the public Big Buck Bunny sample or a direct video link to Kodi. The projector retrieves and plays the video itself.

Prepare the projector

  1. Open Kodi. In Settings → Services → Control, enable Allow remote control via HTTP. Set a username and password, and note the port (usually 8080).
  2. Find the projector’s IP address in Settings → System information → Network. Keep Kodi open and connect the Mac to the same home network.

Connect to Kodi

Enter the projector’s private IPv4 address, beginning with 10., 172.16–31., or 192.168. This controller does not save credentials.

Enter the Kodi settings to connect.

Playback

Paste the direct video link copied from the player. The projector must be able to access it. Links are not saved.

No receiver connected.

After picture and sound start, close this page to check that playback continues. The final phone test will require powering the phone fully off while the projector keeps playing over its own network connection. This Mac test does not establish phone compatibility.

Subtitles

Connect to Kodi, then refresh subtitles while a video is playing.

Optional: paste a direct subtitle link the projector can access. Links are not saved. After loading, refresh subtitles to check the available tracks.
