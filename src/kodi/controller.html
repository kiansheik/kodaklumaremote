Kodi receiver test

Send a public Big Buck Bunny video to Kodi on your projector. The projector retrieves and plays the video itself.

Prepare the projector

  1. Open Kodi. In Settings → Services → Control, enable Allow remote control via HTTP. Set a username and password, and note the port (usually 8080).
  2. Find the projector’s IP address in Settings → System information → Network. Keep Kodi open and connect the Mac to the same home network.

Connect to Kodi

Enter the projector’s private IPv4 address, beginning with 10., 172.16–31., or 192.168. This controller does not save credentials.

Enter the Kodi settings to connect.
